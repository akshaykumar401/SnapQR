Powerful Capabilities

Engineered for Modern Brands & Creators

Everything you need to deliver engaging QR scans and memorable loading splash transitions.

Custom Loading Message

Greet scanners with branded splash screens, custom messages, announcements, or discount codes before reaching the destination.

High Error Correction

Built with level-H Reed-Solomon error correction so your QR code scans flawlessly even with embedded logos or minor surface damage.

Brand Customization

Customize module colors, card titles, and center logo emblems with crisp transparent boundaries and auto-contrast.

Print-Ready Vector Exports

Download ultra-high definition PNG (up to 2048px) or scale-free SVG vector graphics ready for billboards, menus, or business cards.

Instant Live Preview

Dual-mode studio preview simulates both the physical QR code appearance and the mobile scan splash experience in real time.

Zero Tracking & Private

Generate clean, direct, or redirected links with full privacy. No intrusive telemetry or forced expiration dates.
